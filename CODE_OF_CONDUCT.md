# Code of Conduct

DBZ LSW Fangame is a technical fan-project community. Participation requires respectful, evidence-focused collaboration.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat contributors with respect regardless of experience level or background.
- Critique code, evidence and technical claims rather than people.
- Distinguish confirmed findings from hypotheses.
- Give credit for research, testing and implementation work.
- Keep reviews specific, actionable and relevant to the change.
- Respect copyright, licensing and the project's public-source boundaries.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or targeted hostility.
- Personal attacks or sustained bad-faith disruption.
- Publishing private information about another person.
- Intentionally submitting ROMs, proprietary dumps or other material prohibited by project policy.
- Falsifying evidence or presenting speculation as verified ROM behaviour after correction.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its contributors. Moderation decisions should be proportionate to the behaviour and documented where practical.

For security-sensitive reports, follow `SECURITY.md` rather than opening a public issue.
